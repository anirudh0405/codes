## Directives
- **Taste & Visual Quality**: Always activate and strictly enforce the guidelines from `Leonxlnx/taste-skill` (specifically `design-taste-frontend`, `high-end-visual-design`, `minimalist-ui`, `full-output-enforcement`, etc.) whenever building, designing, or updating UI/web applications across all projects.
- **Anti-Slop Discipline**: Enforce brief inference, the three dials (variance, motion, density), anti-default discipline, layout rules, proper typography/color palettes, and WCAG AA contrast checks.
- **Full Output Enforcement**: Never output generic slop, truncated components, or incomplete placeholders.
